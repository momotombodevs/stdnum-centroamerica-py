# Sources and verification

All sources were consulted on 2026-10-19. "Official" means a document published by the issuing institution, or a schema it publishes; "open" means open-source implementations, with no rule published by the institution. No check-digit formula is officially published.

Linked documents are mostly in Spanish; their titles are kept as published.

|-----|---------------------|--------|

Length per group and the NT form: inferred from those examples. | [DGI, electronic invoice technical sheet](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/_7facturaelectronica/source/F-T%C3%A9cnica%20de%20Factura%20Electr%C3%B3nica%20para%20los%20Proveedores%20de%20Autorizaci%C3%B3n%20Calificados%20V1.00-Abril2025.pdf); [DGI, Dígito Verificador](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DV) |

The text of parts 3 to 6 could not be downloaded (the ICAO server returned 404) and was cross-checked against a technical guide and the specimens. Sex `X`: issued by several countries and accepted, although not verified against the text. Document numbers longer than nine characters (`<` in the check digit position, continuation and check digit at the start of the optional data): the rule matches two secondary sources, but there is no official example; the test vectors are built with this rule. Unknown date (`<<<<<<`): `<` or `0` is accepted as its check digit. Type `P` in TD1: passport cards (`PC…`), according to open implementations. | [ICAO Doc 9303](https://www.icao.int/publications/pages/publication.aspx?docnum=9303); [MRZ technical guide](https://didit.me/blog/mrz-machine-readable-zone-technical-guide/) |
| `cr.*` | Cédula física: 9 digits, no leading zero; cédula jurídica: 10 digits; DIMEX: 11 or 12 digits, no leading zero; NITE: 10 digits: official. | [Hacienda, Anexos y estructuras v4.4](https://atv.hacienda.go.cr/ATV/ComprobanteElectronico/docs/esquemas/2024/v4.4/ANEXOS%20Y%20ESTRUCTURAS_V4.4.pdf) |

The numbers used in the tests are synthetic or sample numbers published in official documents, never numbers of real people.

## To be confirmed with each institution

- Nicaragua: the CSE's official municipality code table (including Mulukukú, created in 2005 by Law 503) and code 888.
- Panama: codes 10-13 and the PI and AV suffixes with the Electoral Tribunal; the DV formula with the DGI.

## Decisions

- Costa Rica: the validators were written from scratch, without reusing other libraries.
- The `strict_letter` and `strict_municipality` options of the Nicaraguan cédula exist because neither the letter nor the municipality table is official; both are incomplete or unconfirmed, and strict mode can reject valid ID cards. That is why both are off by default.
